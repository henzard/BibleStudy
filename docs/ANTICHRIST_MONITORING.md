## 🎯 Our Bible-Only Approach

### ✅ What We DO:

1. **Monitor Observable Characteristics**
   - Track patterns that *resemble* biblical descriptions
   - Use keywords from Scripture (e.g., "exalts himself", "sits in temple", "denies Father and Son")
   - Cross-verify with Tier 1 sources (Reuters, AP, BBC)

2. **Map to Biblical Categories**
   - MS0: Man of sin characteristics (2 Thess 2:3-4)
   - MS1: Lying signs and wonders (2 Thess 2:9-12)
   - AC0: Denies Father and Son (1 John 2:22)
   - B1: Beast from sea (Rev 13:1-10)
   - B2: Beast from earth (Rev 13:11-18)

3. **Use Cautious Language**
   - "Resembles category"
   - "Could be consistent with"
   - "Observable pattern"
   - **NOT** "This is that" or "Fulfillment"

### ❌ What We DON'T Do:

1. **Identify Individuals**
   - We do NOT claim "X person is the Antichrist"
   - Scripture describes CHARACTERISTICS, not identity

2. **Speculate on Religion/Ethnicity**
   - We do NOT claim "The Antichrist will be Muslim" or "will be from X religion"
   - Scripture is silent on these details

3. **Claim Fulfillment**
   - We do NOT say "This event fulfills prophecy"
   - We only note "This resembles the category described in [Scripture]"

## 🤔 Could the Antichrist Be Muslim?

### What Scripture Says:

**The Bible does NOT explicitly state:**
- The Antichrist's religion
- The Antichrist's ethnicity
- The Antichrist's nationality
- The Antichrist's name

**The Bible DOES describe:**
- Characteristics (opposes God, exalts himself, sits in temple)
- Actions (blasphemy, war with saints, signs and wonders)
- Outcomes (deception, mark enforced, authority)

### Different Interpretive Perspectives:

1. **Traditional View (European):**
   - Some interpret Rev 13:1-2 (beast from sea) as European
   - Some interpret "ten horns" (Rev 17:12) as European powers
   - **Note:** This is interpretation, not explicit Scripture

2. **Middle Eastern View:**
   - Some interpret Dan 9:27 ("prince that shall come") as Middle Eastern
   - Some note Islamic eschatology parallels (Mahdi, Dajjal)
   - **Note:** This is also interpretation, not explicit Scripture

3. **Our Bible-Only Position:**
   - **We don't know** the Antichrist's religion or ethnicity
   - **We do know** the characteristics Scripture describes
   - **We monitor** observable patterns that resemble these characteristics
   - **We don't claim** fulfillment or identity
